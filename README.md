# SAFIR 5C™: Strategic Agentic Framework for Intelligent Reasoning

**Created by Jérôme Derenne** | **Implemented by [brainTerms.ai](https://brainterms.ai)**



SAFIR 5C™ is an open strategic reasoning framework that organizes Large Language Models into structured executive‑style cognitive roles (Context, Curator, Contributor, Consensus, Command).
It powers the [**brainTerms.ai**](https://brainterms.ai/) platform and enables advanced agentic orchestration for AI‑augmented strategy design.

Unlike a standard LLM, SAFIR 5C™ does not take the model's numbers on trust: its five cognitive layers run inside a **Deterministic Harness**, a neuro‑symbolic architecture that independently verifies the figures behind every strategic conclusion.

In the rapidly evolving landscape of AI-augmented organizations, traditional strategic thinking often falls short. Linear, siloed approaches are no longer sufficient; instead, a dynamic, multi-perspective framework integrated with real-time execution is imperative.

## 2. Architecture Overview: The 5C Cognitive Layers
SAFIR 5C™ is an AI-native orchestration model meticulously engineered to emulate and amplify the cognitive processes of high-performing executive teams. It moves beyond generic AI answers to orchestrate a sophisticated interplay of roles, facilitated dialogue, and robust consensus-building.

### Core Layers
* **Context Layer:** Frames and clarifies the strategic challenge through interactive problem scoping. It reframes unstructured user input into precise mission blueprints using a **Strategic Archetype Mapper**.
* **Curator Layer:** Acts as a **"Strategic HR Engine,"** selecting the optimal AI agent team based on functional expertise, industry knowledge, and cognitive styles (e.g., Visionary, Skeptic, Operator).
* **Contributor Layer:** Ensures **"Cognitive Isolation"** by enabling agents to generate autonomous insights independently before any group dialogue, preventing alignment bias or mimicry.
* **Consensus Layer:** Manages a structured **Turn-Based Dialogue Simulator** where agents critique and refine peer work to identify zones of agreement and resolve conflicts.
* **Command Layer:** Translates endorsed decisions into high-fidelity executive deliverables, such as roadmaps and presentations, with a **Justification Mapper** to ensure transparency.



## 3. The Deterministic Harness
A standard LLM produces figures the same way it produces sentences: as plausible text. Nothing recomputes them, checks them against each other, or tests whether the conclusion survives a change in assumptions.

SAFIR 5C™ separates **reasoning** from **verification**. The agents reason, debate and recommend; a deterministic harness around the five layers governs the numbers.

* **Computed, not generated:** The figures that decide a strategy's viability are established by the harness, not written by the model.
* **Cross-checked across experts:** All agents work from one shared set of figures, so finance, operations and technology cannot rest on contradictory assumptions.
* **Rule-based verdicts:** Viability is decided by explicit criteria, not by the model's tone or confidence.
* **Stress-tested conclusions:** Verdicts are challenged under changing assumptions before they are presented.
* **Declared uncertainty:** When key information is missing, the verdict is marked provisional and the gap is named, instead of being filled with a confident guess.

| | Standard LLM | SAFIR 5C™ |
|---|---|---|
| Who establishes the numbers | The model, as text | A deterministic harness |
| Figures checked across roles | No | Yes |
| Basis of the verdict | The model's judgment | Explicit criteria |
| Robustness tested | No | Yes |
| Missing information | Filled with a guess | Declared and named |



## 4. Advanced Features & Safeguards
* **Strategic Traceability:** Every decision, prompt, and interaction is meticulously logged, providing a comprehensive strategic audit trail for full accountability. Every verified figure can also be traced back to its origin.
* **Sub-Agent Modular System:** Core Strategic Agents are supported by specialized sub-agents for **Discussion**, **Report Generation**, and **Slide Generation** to enhance workflow specialization.
* **Causal Simulation:** The framework can run multiple agent constellations to explore divergent futures (e.g., low-margin growth vs. premium segmentation) before committing resources, with each scenario held to the same verification standard.
* **Augmentation Focus:** SAFIR is not designed to replace human leaders but to augment their knowledge, reach, and precision, providing "Synthetic Collective Intelligence".
